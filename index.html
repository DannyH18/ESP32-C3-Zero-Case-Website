About This Project

This project explores the design and development of a protective case for the ESP32-C3 Zero board. The goal is to create a case that is practical, durable, and easy to use while allowing access to ports, buttons, and indicators.

The website is structured to show the evolution of the design through multiple prototypes, highlighting the pros, cons, and planned improvements at each stage, leading to the final design.

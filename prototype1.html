Prototype 1

Initial case design focusing on basic fit and structure for the ESP32-C3 Zero.

Design Description

Prototype 1 was created to test the basic dimensions and fit of the ESP32-C3 Zero board inside a custom case.

Pros

  • Simple and easy design.
  • Simple structure makes it easy to print.
  • Provides a basis for future designs.

Cons

  • Pins on the bottom do not fit within the holes and the buttons on the top cannot be pressed.
  • Lid is too thin to be properly printed.
  • No proper ventilation.

Planned Improvements

  • Improve port access.
  • Make holes on the lid actually give you the ability to press the buttons.
  • Add ventilation slots.
